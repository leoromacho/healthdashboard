Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 13, 2026

Health Dashboard is a personal health & fitness dashboard built on Apple Health. This page explains, in full, what data the app touches and why.

No account, name, or email required
No data ever leaves your device
No analytics, ads, or tracking

What data is collected

With your explicit permission — requested by iOS the first time each feature is used, and always revocable from Settings → Privacy & Security → Health — Health Dashboard reads the following from Apple Health to build your dashboard, readiness score, and exports:

  • Workouts, steps, and other activity data.
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages.
  • Heart rate, heart‑rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, and blood oxygen (SpO₂).
  • Wrist temperature and other mobility/vitals metrics your devices record.

If you use the built‑in pulse‑measurement feature, the app also turns on your phone's rear camera and flash to read your pulse from your fingertip. The video frames are analyzed live, on your device, to compute heart rate and HRV — no image or video is ever stored or transmitted, only the resulting numbers (BPM, HRV, timestamp).

Health Dashboard does not collect your name, email, or any other personally identifiable data. There is no account, no sign‑up, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) — the app never requests it, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt ever appears.

How your data is stored

Health Dashboard has no server or backend of any kind. All processing — reading your Health data, computing scores, running the camera pulse analysis — happens entirely on your iPhone. Camera‑measured readings are saved to a small file inside the app's own local storage, and, if the "Save to Apple Health" setting is on (the default), mirrored back into Health so they sit alongside your watch data. Nothing is ever uploaded to the internet.

If you export a CSV or JSON file, that file is generated locally and handed to the standard iOS share sheet you invoke — you choose where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail, etc.). Health Dashboard itself never transmits it anywhere.

Your Apple Watch

If you install the optional Watch companion app, your iPhone sends a compact summary of your dashboard directly to your own paired Apple Watch, device‑to‑device, using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This transfer never touches the internet, a server, or any third party — it stays between the two devices you own.

Who it's shared with

Nobody. Health Dashboard contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ad network, and no third‑party processor of any kind. Your health data is never sold, never shared, and never transmitted off your device by the app.

Permissions and consent

Reading and writing Health data, and using the camera, each require a separate permission grant from iOS itself — you decide, category by category, the first time a feature needs it. You can review or withdraw any of these permissions at any time from Settings → Privacy & Security → Health or → Camera. Withdrawing a permission simply means the corresponding part of the dashboard shows less — the app never stops working entirely.

How long data is kept

Camera‑measured readings and cached workout data live in the app's local storage for as long as the app stays installed; deleting the app deletes them. Data read from Apple Health is governed by Apple Health itself — Health Dashboard only reads what's there at the time, it doesn't keep its own long‑term copy of your Health records.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected off your device, there is no remote copy of your data to request, correct, or export from us — everything already lives on your iPhone, under your control. You can revoke any permission in Settings at any time, or remove all locally stored app data by deleting the app. For any other question about your data, write to the address below.
